What I built

A racing form guide Alexa+ can call

The server
An MCP server, in Rust
12 tools over Streamable HTTP (MCP 2025-11-25), OAuth 2.1 with PKCE, as the Alexa+ track requires. Results, fields, form, sectionals, carnival guide, next race.
The data
Real Racing Australia data
Fields, results and sectional times from a read-only archive, rebuilt into one snapshot twice a day. Prices are never read: no betting, no odds.
The difference
It remembers you
Your stable of followed horses, your home state and when you last checked, kept across sessions. On a screen it draws its own race card as an MCP App.
