How you use it

Enable it, link it, ask it

1
Enable and link
Add Trackside in the Alexa app and link your account: an ordinary sign-in, OAuth 2.1 through Amazon Cognito. That is what makes the memory yours.
2
Just ask
Who won, who's running, how a horse is going, who ran home fastest. On an Echo Show the answer comes with a race card.
"Alexa, who won the Caulfield Cup, and who ran home fastest?"
3
Follow your horses
"Follow Hellcibell." Next time you ask, Trackside tells you what your horses did while you were away, and when they run next.
"Alexa, how's my stable?"
Alexa+ talks to it as an MCP server · every answer in this demo comes from the live Trackside server
